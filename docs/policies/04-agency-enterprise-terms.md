# Agency & Enterprise Terms

_Version 2026-11-28 · DRAFT for review. Items in [square brackets] need Sleek’s details; have a Nigerian lawyer review before launch._

For agencies and companies with many workers across trades: business verification, staff responsibility and penalties.

## Includes the Artisan and Team Terms

- All Artisan Terms & Rules and Team Account Terms apply to the agency and every worker on it.

## Business verification

- Sleek asks for your CAC registration number (RC or BN) and the details of a director or owner, who passes full identity verification and gives a guarantor.
- If you are not registered yet you can still apply; the account is flagged and Sleek may cap the number of active jobs until registration is confirmed.
- We recommend public liability insurance for agencies. Sleek Protection does not replace your own insurance.

## Staff and penalties

- The agency is responsible for its staff’s conduct and quality on Sleek jobs. Strikes, lateness and no-show penalties apply to the agency account as well as the worker.
- Repeated unverified workers on jobs, or fraud by staff, can lead to removal of the whole agency account.
- Penalties are Sleek’s standard defaults. Sleek may change them with at least 14 days’ notice in the app and by email. Sleek applies them consistently, using the job timeline, chat history, location check-ins and photos, and you can appeal any penalty through Support within 7 days.
